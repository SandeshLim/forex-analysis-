# Why price reversed before B2 (27 Nov 2026, 18:20 UK, OANDA:XAUUSD)

## What happened
- **15:53–15:54:** a fast drop pierced B1 (3621.2–3626.9) and ran stops down to 3616.1. That was 3.9 points short of the Asia midpoint (3612.2) and short of B2. The same 1m candle closed back inside the zone, at 3622.2.
- **Failed-break buy trigger:** the sweep candle (15:54) had a high of 3622.2, and the 15:55 candle closed at 3624.2, above it. Stop: 3615.6. Risk: 8.6.
- **After entry:** the higher low was 3618.6 at 16:19, so the stop held. The 2R target of 3641.4 was reached at 17:45, and the high later reached 3655.9 (+3.6R). Result: **+2R**.
- **S3 sell (3638.5–3645.9):** price reached 3640.5 inside the zone, with no sweep beyond the top, so there was no trigger. It was broken by the 17:45 15m close at 3648.2.

## Why it turned at B1, not B2
1. **The 4H trend was up:** the BOS was confirmed at 06:00. A 4H uptrend pullback normally holds at the base the rally started from, which is B1 (the 02:45–04:45 Asia base). A pullback does not need to retrace to the midpoint.
2. **It was a liquidity sweep:** the stops below the 14:30 low (3625.2) and below B1 were taken in one minute, then the level was reclaimed immediately. That is stop-hunt behaviour, not real selling.
3. **Timing and liquidity:** the sweep came just before the 16:00 London close, on the thin post-Thanksgiving Friday.
4. **The Asia-midpoint statistic is a tendency, not a rule:** with today included, Set B is now 7 of 8, and today missed by 4.0 points.

## Backtest: plain touch versus sweep-and-reclaim
Data: 1H base zones, 15m bars, 14 Sep – 27 Nov, first touch only, target 2R.

| Entry | With 4H trend | Against 4H trend | All |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain limit at zone edge, stop beyond far edge + 0.5 | n79, 25% won, −19R | n86, 30% won, −8R | −27R |
| Sweep beyond far edge, then a 15m close back inside; stop at sweep extreme ± 0.5 | n40, 40% won, +8R | n33, 42% won, +9R | **n73, 41% won, +17R** |

**Conclusion:** the zone is the place, and the sweep is the signal. Entering on the first touch loses money in this sample; waiting for the sweep and reclaim turns the same zones positive. This matches today, where a plain B1 entry would have been stopped out, while the sweep-and-reclaim entry won 2R.

## Rule change (proposed)
- Do not use a limit order at the zone edge.
- Wait for price to sweep beyond the far edge and close back inside. On 1m, also require a higher low (for buys) or a lower high (for sells), then a close beyond the sweep candle.
- Stop: sweep extreme ± 0.5.
- A 15m close beyond the far edge cancels the zone only if no trigger has fired yet.
